- **Cascade** — projectiles gain +1 bounce with no damage falloff per chain. Dead weight
  until a Bounce affix exists; recommend farming as Orfeo first if the user has none.
- **Solara** — kills during ability uptime cut the next cooldown by 0.5s. Active is
  Firestorm. The payoff is keeping the Ascended trait (+30% damage / reload / attack speed
  for 5s after each ability) permanently up, which needs heavy Blazing investment.
- **Orfeo** — +25% Ash from kills in aura range, +15% XP globally. Active is Resonance
  Pulse (2s stun). The progression character: farm here, spend at the Forge.
- **Vex** — Cold hits have a 30% chance to freeze for 1.2s. Active is Blizzard (-60% enemy
  move speed, 8s). The safe answer for a user dying repeatedly.

## Weapon classes

- **Dual SMG** — fastest fire rate, best proc-per-second, weak per bullet and poor into
  armored elites. The status-effect chassis (Burn or Cold).
- **Shotgun** — multi-pellet, so one blast can proc several effects at once. Best burst,
  needs point-blank range, punishing reloads late.
- **Revolver** — highest per-shot damage, lowest fire rate. With Bounce affixes it clears
  screens; without them it is mediocre.
- **Tesla Gun** — chain lightning with long, vulnerable reloads. Highest ceiling, needs
  late-game affix support to function at all.

## Runes

Recommended unlock order: **Snail → Bloodlust → Priest → Finisher → Giant**, then
character-specific ones. There is no respec — universal runes before niche ones.

- **Snail** — all enemies 15% slower; stacks additively with Cold slows.
- **Bloodlust** — 0.5% lifesteal per kill for 10s, stacking to 15%.
- **Priest** — shrine effects last 50% longer, 25% shorter cooldown.
- **Finisher** — kills below 20% HP deal double damage and drop 2x Ash.
- **Giant** — +40% max HP, +10 HP/sec regen.
- **Frostbite** — Cold hits have a 20% freeze chance; frozen enemies shatter for bonus Ash.
- **Chaotic Bullets** — every 5th bullet deals 300% damage and gains +2 bounce.
- **Arsonist** — +30% Burn damage; burning enemies spread fire on death.
- **Blazing** — +40% ability damage, -2s ability cooldown.
- **Vitality** — 5% chance on kill to restore 3% of max HP.

## The Forge (between runs)

- **Infuse** — 5 weapons of the *same type* → 1 of the next rarity. Affixes are drawn from
  all five sources, so stacking a desired affix in the inputs biases the result. Rarity is
  the primary multiplier: **Infuse before Improve, always.** Not worth it on Legendaries.
- **Improve** — spend Ash for weapon levels at rising fail risk (roughly 5% at levels 1–3,
  15% at 4–6, 30% at 7–9, 50% at 10+). A failure wipes accumulated levels, not the weapon.
  **Stop at level 6 below Epic rarity.**
- **Reforge** — reroll all stats and affixes; cost climbs per attempt on the same weapon.
  Cannot change weapon type. Budget 1–3 attempts unless the weapon is Legendary.
- **Recycle** — dismantle for Ash, scaling with rarity (Common ~10–20 up to Legendary
  ~500–800). Recycle duplicates immediately; never hold more than two of a type.

Forge visit order: recycle duplicates → Infuse any set of five → Improve the primary
weapon if it is Rare+ and below level 6 → Reforge only an Epic/Legendary with bad affixes
→ spend the rest on runes.

## Biomes

| Biome | Difficulty | Elite / Boss | Hazard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Burned Forest | 1–3 | Ash Titan / The Burned Warden | Charge attacks |
| Scorched City | 3–6 | Buff Mage Captain / The Corrupted Architect | Urban chokepoints |
| Corrupted Lands | 6–10 | Corrupted Sorcerer / The Devourer | Toxic detonations |

Priority targets, in every biome: **Healing Mages** (they sustain whole packs) and **Buff
Mages** (damage and speed aura). Kill them the instant they spawn. Dodge is the only
defensive tool — there is no armor or shield — so Dodge Shrines are worth detouring for.
Lifesteal becomes mandatory in Corrupted Lands. The final boss scales with kills, so clear
minions before committing.

## Build archetypes

- **Legendary Revolver Bounce** (S, Cascade) — Bounce + Crit Chance + Crit Damage, with
  Chaotic Bullets / Finisher / Bloodlust. The end-game target.
- **Solara Tesla Ascended** (S, Solara) — Chain + Attack Speed + Crit Chance on an Epic+
  Tesla Gun, with Blazing / Bloodlust / Arsonist. Highest DPS, highest skill floor.
- **Vex SMG Freeze Tank** (A, Vex) — Cold + Attack Speed + Lifesteal, with Frostbite /
  Snail / Giant / Bloodlust. The forgiving way into high difficulty.
- **Orfeo Shotgun Ash Farm** (A, Orfeo) — Burn + Lifesteal + AOE, with Priest / Finisher /
  Vitality. Not a combat build; the engine that funds the others.

Builds do not mix weapon types — Infuse requires five identical weapons, so one character
and one weapon class per build.
